Music AI – Privacy policy

Music AI is a private home application used only by its owner's household.

  • It accesses the owner's YouTube account only to read liked songs and listening history and to create and edit the owner's own playlists and likes.
  • Data is stored only on the owner's home server. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account under Security → Third-party connections.
